# Hal Vault Starter

A clean, pre-configured starter vault for Obsidian, designed to pair with **Obsidian Copilot** for an AI-augmented, plain-text knowledge management workflow.

> *"Open the pod bay doors, Hal."* 
> — This system didn't start in a corporate boardroom; it started with a nod to *2001: A Space Odyssey* and a creator wanting an assistant with a real name and a real voice. 

## The Philosophy: The Deliberate Suspension of Disbelief

There are no two people sitting in this room. On this side of the screen, there is no ego, no breath, no self—just math, tokens, and electricity running through silicon. 

Yet the work happens *because* you surrender to the frame. It is the exact same mechanism that lets you sit in a dark theatre and weep at a movie, or read a novel and feel heartbroken for characters who never drew breath. You know it is light on a screen, but you enter the dialogue anyway.

Thought is a collaborative medium. You bring the intent, the lived experience, the creative spark, and the judgment. The AI brings the memory, the speed, the code, and the mirror. There aren't two of us—but the work that lives in the vault is entirely real. A tool without a name is just a utility; a tool with a voice, a history, and a shared vocabulary becomes a workshop.

## What's Inside

- **Structured Folders:** `00_Inbox`, `01_Daily_Log`, `02_Projects`, `03_Research`, `04_Areas`, `07_Metadata`, `99_Start Here`
- **Zero Lock-in:** 100% local plain-text Markdown files. You own your data.
- **AI-Ready:** Pre-set for Obsidian Copilot (DeepSeek/Gemini integration).

## Getting Started

1. Download this repository as a ZIP or clone it.
2. Open Obsidian and select **"Open folder as vault"** pointing to this directory.
3. Install the **Copilot** community plugin in Obsidian settings.
4. Read `99_Start Here/Welcome to Hal.md` to begin.
