# Welcome to Hal — Your AI-Augmented Vault

> [!info] What this is
> You are looking at a plain-text knowledge management system paired with a local AI assistant. It isn't Notion, it isn't Evernote, and it's not trapped in a cloud provider's walled garden. Every note here is a standard Markdown file stored right on your hard drive. You own it, you control it, and it works for pennies.

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy

- **Capture raw, process clean:** Don't spend hours organising your thoughts when you have them. Dump everything into your `00_Inbox` or today's daily log. Let the AI help you sort it later.
- **The Daily Log is the anchor:** Every day gets one note (`YYYY-MM-DD.md` in `01_Daily_Log`). That is your timeline, your running journal, and your working memory. 
- **An augmented brain, not a second brain:** You are the thinker (4±1 working memory chunks); the vault is the long-term memory; the AI is the associative processor.

---

## 2. How to Set It Up (Takes ~15 Minutes)

1. **Download Obsidian:** If you don't already have it, grab it free from [obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md).
2. **Open this folder as a vault:** Launch Obsidian, click "Open folder as vault", and select this `hal-vault-starter` folder.
3. **Install Obsidian Copilot:** Go to Settings → Community plugins → Browse → search for **Copilot** and install/enable it.
4. **Add an API key:** Read **[[Set Up Your API Key (DeepSeek) - the 10-minute start]]** and follow it — it teaches you how to get a DeepSeek key and plug it in. This is the step that makes the vault talk. (It's in the reading list below too.)
5. **Say hello + load in:** Open the Copilot chat panel, type **"full load-in"**, and let Hal read the Start Here notes and orient to your vault. Then ask **"what's next?"** to get a single suggested action.

## 3. Folder Layout

- `00_Inbox/` — Landing bay for raw clippings, web quotes, and random thoughts.
- `01_Daily_Log/` — Your daily chronological record. Live here.
- `02_Projects/` — Active work with a clear definition of "done" and an Action Log.
- `03_Research/` — Curiosity items, reference material, and deep reads.
- `04_Areas/` — Ongoing domains of responsibility (finances, health, business areas).
- `05_Archive/` — Superseded notes and retired automations, kept but out of the way.
- `06_Attachments/` — Images and media.
- `07_Metadata/` — System handovers, credentials pointers, and configuration notes.
- `99_Start Here/` — Guides and onboarding notes (like this one).

---

## 4. Give Hal the Full Access Stack

Hal is most useful when it can reach your machine. Read these in order:

1. **[[Set Up Your API Key (DeepSeek) - the 10-minute start]]** — get a key, plug it in, say hello. *Do this first — nothing talks until it's done.*
2. **[[Permissions Setup - give Hal hands, eyes, ears and voice]]** — grant hands, eyes, ears, voice (Accessibility, Screen Recording, Safari JS, mic). *Do this once the key works — it turns the vault into an operating system.*
3. **[[Capability Map]]** — everything Hal can do once the permissions are on.
4. **[[Hal Workflows]]** — the master patterns (how we work together: write → clipboard → paste → Hal wires it up).
5. **[[Operating Workflows - the nitty-gritty mechanics]]** — the mechanical detail (clipboard, Safari text injection, screen-reading, clicking).
6. **[[Keeping the System Healthy - Resilience and the Incident Playbook]]** — read this once things are running: health checks, versioned backups, and the calm way to handle a "it all looks gone" moment. (Learned the hard way — don't skip it.)
7. **[[Voice Capture - dictate freely, consolidate at load-in]]** — the voice-dictation capture model.

> The short version of the whole system: **capture raw, process clean, and let Hal do the plumbing.** The clipboard is the handshake — if Hal says "it's on your clipboard," you just paste.
